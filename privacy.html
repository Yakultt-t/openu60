SIM 随行把你的 iPhone 和你自己的中兴 U60 Pro 路由器连在一起，用路由器里的 SIM 卡收发短信、接打电话。

  • 没有账号，不需要注册。
  • 没有广告、没有统计分析、不跟踪你。
  • 不出售、不分享你的任何数据。

你的短信、通话和设置都在你的路由器和你的设备之间传递。下面写明哪些数据会经过网络、经过谁的服务器。

只保存在你设备上的数据

  • 短信和彩信：App 在手机上保存一份路由器里的短信，方便离线查看。最近删除的短信保留 30 天。
  • 通话记录和通话录音：只在你主动录音时保存在 iPhone 上。通话记录会通过你自己的 iCloud 在你的设备之间同步。
  • 录音转文字和 AI 摘要：全部用 Apple 的本机语音识别和 Apple 智能在 iPhone 上生成，不上传。
  • 通讯录：只在手机上读取，用来显示号码对应的名字，不上传、不保存副本。
  • 路由器密码和配对密钥：保存在钥匙串里。打开 iCloud 钥匙串时，会在你自己的设备之间同步。

经过网络的数据

手机和路由器之间

在同一个网络里时，手机直接连路由器。在外面时，经过开发者运营的中继服务器转发。

转发的内容是端到端加密的：两端互相核对密钥，中继服务器只看得到加密后的数据包、双方的公钥和 IP 地址，看不到短信、通话或任何设置内容。

推送通知

路由器把通知请求发给开发者运营的推送网关，再由网关交给 Apple 推送通知服务（APNs）送到你的设备。不同通知包含的内容：

  • 短信通知：发件号码和最多 500 字的短信正文。
  • 来电通知：来电号码。
  • 设备提醒：路由器状态，比如电量、离线。

网关保存的东西：发送通知所需的设备推送令牌和路由器公钥，以及发送成功或失败的记录。

网关不保存通知内容。你可以在 App 的「设置 → 通知」或 iOS 设置里关闭任何一种通知。

路由器离线提醒和 Apple Watch

  • 路由器每分钟向网关报一次平安，只带路由器的编号，网关据此在它离线时通知你。
  • Apple Watch 不在 iPhone 旁边时，经过网关中转，内容同样端到端加密。

App 请求的权限

  • 麦克风：通话时把你的声音传给路由器。
  • 本地网络：在家里或路由器的 Wi-Fi 里找到路由器。
  • 通讯录：显示联系人名字。
  • 相机、照片：拍照发彩信、扫描路由器的 Wi-Fi 二维码、保存彩信图片。
  • 语音识别：在本机把通话录音转成文字。
  • 面容 ID：查看保存的路由器密码，以及应用锁。
  • 通知：短信、来电和设备提醒。

删除你的数据

  • 在 App 里删除短信、录音或通话记录，就会从手机上删除。
  • 退出登录会清除手机上保存的短信副本。
  • 在「设置 → 通知」里关掉通知，路由器就不再给这台设备发推送。
  • 在「设置 → 设备与配对」里移除设备，这台设备就不能再连接路由器。
  • 删除 App 会删除它在这台设备上的所有数据。

Privacy Policy

SIM Go · Last updated 11 November 2026

SIM Go connects your iPhone to your own ZTE U60 Pro router so you can use the SIM card in it for messages and calls.

  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We never sell or share your data.

Your messages, calls and settings travel between your router and your devices. This page says what passes over the network and whose servers it touches.

Data kept only on your devices

  • Text and picture messages: a copy of the router's messages is kept on the phone so you can read them offline. Recently deleted messages are kept for 30 days.
  • Call history and call recordings: recordings are saved on the iPhone only when you record a call. Call history syncs between your devices through your own iCloud.
  • Transcripts and AI summaries: made on the iPhone with Apple's on-device speech recognition and Apple Intelligence. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Contacts: read on the device to show names. They are never uploaded or copied.
  • Router password and pairing keys: kept in the Keychain. With iCloud Keychain on, they sync between your own devices.

Data that crosses the network

Between your phone and your router

On the same network, the phone connects directly. Away from it, traffic goes through a relay server run by the developer.

That traffic is end-to-end encrypted, with each side checking the other's key. The relay sees only encrypted packets, the two public keys and IP addresses, never message, call or settings content.

Push notifications

The router sends notification requests to a push gateway run by the developer, which hands them to Apple Push Notification service (APNs). What each kind contains:

  • Message notifications: the sender's number and up to 500 characters of the text.
  • Call notifications: the caller's number.
  • Device alerts: the router's status, such as battery or offline.

The gateway keeps the device push tokens and router public keys it needs, and a log of deliveries. It does not keep notification content. Turn off any kind of notification in the app's Settings → Notifications or in iOS Settings.

Router offline alerts and Apple Watch

  • The router checks in with the gateway once a minute, sending only its ID, so you can be told when it goes offline.
  • When the Apple Watch is away from the iPhone, it reaches the router through the gateway, also end-to-end encrypted.

Permissions

  • Microphone: carries your voice to the router during calls.
  • Local network: finds the router at home or on its Wi-Fi.
  • Contacts: shows contact names.
  • Camera and photos: takes photos for picture messages, scans the router's Wi-Fi QR code and saves message photos.
  • Speech recognition: transcribes call recordings on the device.
  • Face ID: protects the saved router password and the app lock.
  • Notifications: messages, calls and device alerts.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting messages, recordings or call history in the app removes them from the phone.
  • Signing out clears the saved copy of your messages.
  • Turning notifications off under Settings → Notifications stops the router from sending pushes to that device.
  • Removing a device under Settings → Devices & Pairing stops it from connecting to the router.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
